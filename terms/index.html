Triarchy Messaging Terms

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Program

Triarchy is a personal AI assistant app operated by Johnathan Mendes. Its assistants (Harvey, Grant and Rhodes) communicate only with the account owner: by messages on Telegram and by voice calls with Harvey at (689) 407-5448.

Who it's for

Triarchy is a private tool. The assistants accept messages only from the owner's Telegram account and calls only from the owner's phone number. Messages and calls from anyone else are ignored.

What the owner receives

Updates on tasks the owner asked the assistants to do, requests for approval before actions are taken, reminders, and replies to the owner's messages.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies with use. Standard data rates from your carrier or internet provider may apply.

Stopping messages

Send /stop to an assistant in Telegram, or block the bot, to stop its messages. Contact support@gettriarchy.com for help.

Third-party services

Messages are delivered through Telegram and calls through Twilio, under their own terms. Neither is responsible for delayed or undelivered messages from Triarchy.
