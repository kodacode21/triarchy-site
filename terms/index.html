Triarchy Messaging Terms

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Program

Triarchy (Harvey) personal assistant alerts, operated by Johnathan Mendes. Messages come from Harvey, a personal AI assistant in the Triarchy app, and are sent only to the account owner who opted in.

How to opt in

Text START to (689) 407-5448 from your mobile phone. You will receive a confirmation message.

What you'll receive

Updates on tasks you asked the assistant to do, requests for your approval before actions are taken, reminders, and replies to messages you send.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your use of the assistant.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opting out

Reply HELP for help or contact support@gettriarchy.com. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages; you will receive one final confirmation message.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy at https://gettriarchy.com/privacy.
