Triarchy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Triarchy is a personal AI assistant app operated by Johnathan Mendes ("we," "us"). This policy explains how we handle information, including information related to our text messaging program.

Information we collect

We collect the mobile phone number that opts in to Triarchy text messages, the content of messages sent to and from that number, and basic records of when messages were sent and delivered.

How we use it

We use this information only to send and receive the assistant messages you requested (task updates, approval requests, reminders and replies), to respond to HELP and STOP requests, and to keep the service working and secure.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We use service providers (such as our text messaging carrier) only to deliver messages, and they may use the information only for that purpose.

Opting out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.

Security and retention

We protect information with reasonable security measures and keep message records only as long as needed to operate the service.
