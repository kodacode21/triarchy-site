Triarchy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Triarchy is a personal AI assistant app operated by Johnathan Mendes ("we," "us"). This policy explains how we handle information, including messages on Telegram and voice calls.

Information we collect

We collect the owner's Telegram account ID and phone number, the content of messages and calls with the assistants, and basic records of when they happened.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the assistants: to complete tasks the owner asks for, send updates, approval requests and reminders, answer the owner's messages and calls, and keep the service working and secure.

Sharing

We do not sell this information or share it with anyone for marketing or promotional purposes. We use service providers only to deliver the service: Telegram (messages), Twilio (voice calls), and AI model providers (Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI), which process message and call content to generate the assistants' replies. They may use the information only for that purpose, under their own policies.

Security and retention

We protect information with reasonable security measures, including encryption, and keep records only as long as needed to operate the service.
